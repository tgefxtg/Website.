02 CORE SERVICES

The Heart of
Our Escapes.

Kerala Backwater & Houseboat Experiences

Glide through Kerala's emerald waterways on a traditional, hand-crafted houseboat.

Discover the slow-paced rhythm of rural Kerala on our exclusive backwater cruises. From tranquil day trips to overnight stays on premium houseboats, you'll wake up to the sounds of water birds and the sight of endless palm-fringed shores.

  • What to Expect: Private houseboat stays, local Kerala cuisine onboard, village walks.
  • Destinations: Alappuzha (Alleppey), Kumarakom, Kollam.

Hill Station & Tea Garden Retreats

Breathe in the crisp mountain air amidst endless rolling tea estates.

Escape the tropical heat and journey into the cool, cloud-kissed altitudes of the Western Ghats. Our hill station retreats offer a blend of colonial-era charm, sweeping valley views, and unhurried mornings.

  • What to Expect: Heritage bungalow stays, tea factory tours, early morning viewpoints.
  • Destinations: Munnar, Wayanad, Vagamon.

Wildlife & Nature Expeditions

Step into the wild and witness Kerala's incredible biodiversity up close.

Journey deep into dense rainforests and protected sanctuaries teeming with wildlife. Whether you are tracking elephants along spice-scented trails or bamboo rafting on a quiet lake, our nature expeditions bring you face-to-face with Kerala's untamed beauty.

  • What to Expect: Jeep safaris, guided jungle walks, bamboo rafting.
  • Destinations: Thekkady (Periyar), Silent Valley.
